% For the latter half of the twentieth century, Heads-Up Display (HUD) technology emerged as a means of enhancing the experience of operators of military aircraft.

% In this paper, we identify a practical use case of XR technology, for generating an intuitive HUD for transport and mobile equipment operation purposes. In conjunction with a data aggregation service, an enhanced operating experience can be realized, and made available to the user. This would allow them to have additional information available, within sight, that may benefit their operating experience.

% The system is designed to be extensible, eventually supporting a wide array of modes of transportation (land, air, water), as well as mobile equipment in agriculture and industrial settings. For the purposes of this thesis, we will be exploring automotive applications only.
