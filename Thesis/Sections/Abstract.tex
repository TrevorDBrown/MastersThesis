\par Since the advent of the modern automobile, manufacturers have provided means of tracking various critical data points associated with automobile operation, with the most prominent and standardized method being the instrument cluster. These data points include, but are not limited to, automobile speed (i.e. \gls{mph}), engine speed (i.e. \gls{rpm}), fuel level, and battery charge (i.e. Volts). While this data is updated in real-time as the automobile is running, traditional instrument clusters cannot be modified or adjusted to the automobile driver's needs.

\par Alongside automobile instrumentation, the \gls{hud} evolved from a rudimentary targeting mechanism on firearms and military implements, to advanced electronic systems in military aircraft, to its inclusion in some automobiles. \Glspl{hud} provide a dynamic interface which overlays onto a visible field of operation.

\par In this paper, a dynamic, \gls{ar}-based software system is proposed, which combines the visual elements found in aircraft and dashboard-embedded \glspl{hud}, and the data points displayed in traditional automobile instrumentation. Furthermore, information not typically included in automobile instrumentation can be included through customizations made by the end user.

\par The object of this solution, named ``DashAR'', is to provide automobile drivers the opportunity to use the dynamic nature of the solution to create customized \gls{hud} layouts, tailored to their specific needs.

\noindent Keywords: augmented reality, heads up display, automobile, instrumentation, user experience
