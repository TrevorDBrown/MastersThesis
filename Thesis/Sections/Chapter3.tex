\par DashAR (pronounced either as it looks (a portmanteau of ``Dashboard'' and ``\gls{ar}''), or like Dasher the Reindeer) is a dynamic \gls{hud} solution for drivers of automobiles. It provides drivers near real-time information, such as automobile's speed in space, the engine's rotational speed internally, the amount of fuel remaining in the fuel tank, and so on. These are pieces of information generally found on the instrument cluster of any modern automobile.

\par The first question that may come to someone's mind when thinking about this is: ``Why can't I just look down at the instrument cluster in my car?''. For some information, you definitely could do that. After all, it is there to help the driver. However, the biggest issue is the information presented is fixed. The instrument cluster can't be adjusted to meet the needs of every individual that operates a specific automobile. The information presented within an instrument cluster is also at the mercy of the manufacturer.
